Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026
Last Updated: September 26, 2026

StudyMate AI helps students create summaries and practice questions from PDF study materials. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it. It follows Pakistan's Personal Data Protection Bill 2023 and international privacy standards.

What information we collect

Personal information
- Your name, email address, and account details
- Information from your login provider
- Your activity and usage logs

Study materials
- PDF files you upload
- Text we extract from your PDFs
- Summaries and quiz questions we generate for you
- File names, sizes, and upload dates

Technical information
- Your IP address and device type
- Browser and operating system information
- Pages you visit and how long you stay
- Error logs needed to fix problems
- Cookie data for keeping you logged in

How we use your information

We use your information to:
- Create and manage your account
- Verify your identity securely
- Store and process your uploaded documents
- Generate summaries and quiz questions using AI
- Provide customer support
- Prevent fraud and abuse
- Follow Pakistan's legal requirements
- Improve the platform and user experience

AI and third parties

When you upload a PDF:
- We extract text from it
- We send this text to Claude AI to create summaries and quizzes
- Claude processes your data only to generate the output you requested
- Claude may be located outside Pakistan
- Claude has its own privacy policy and data retention rules

Do not upload confidential or sensitive content unless you have permission.

How long we keep your data

- Uploaded PDFs and generated content: kept while your account is active, then deleted 30 days after account closure
- Technical logs and error records: kept for 90 days
- Account information: deleted within 30 days after account closure, except data required by law

Your rights

You can:
- Ask for a copy of your personal data we hold
- Ask us to correct wrong information
- Ask us to delete your account and data
- Get your data in a portable format
- Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, email:
privacy@studymate-ai.com

Data security

We protect your data using:
- HTTPS encryption
- Access controls and passwords
- Server-side validation of uploaded files
- Private storage with restricted permissions
- Regular security monitoring

No system is 100% secure. Do not upload passwords, payment card numbers, ID card numbers, or highly confidential personal information.

Sharing your information

We share information with:
- Cloud hosting providers
- Database providers
- Storage providers
- AI providers
- Pakistan authorities when required by law
- Law enforcement to prevent illegal activity

We do NOT:
- Sell your data to advertisers or marketers
- Share your academic content between users
- Disclose your data without your consent

International transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside Pakistan. We protect it with appropriate safeguards.

Cookies

We use cookies to:
- Keep you logged in
- Remember your preferences
- Track how you use the platform
- Improve the user experience

You can turn off cookies in your browser, but some features may not work properly.

Children

StudyMate AI is not for children under 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.
